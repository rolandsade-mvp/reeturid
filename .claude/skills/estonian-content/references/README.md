# Drop zone for Estonian reference material

Put anything here that should shape the Estonian in this repo: articles you like, reviewed drafts with comments, terminology lists. Any format works; a one-line note at the top saying WHY it is here multiplies its value. The estonian-content skill reads this folder before writing.

The two `notes-*.md` files and `owner-links.md` are copied from the Mealhive skill (`rolandsade-mvp/meal-planner`, `.claude/skills/estonian-content/references/`), where the 16 source PDFs they were distilled from also live. Mealhive is the master copy of the general rules.
